Agro V - Features Roadmap

Version 1.0 - Plant Health Assistant

Disease Detection

- Upload a picture of a plant leaf
- Detect common plant diseases
- Display disease name
- Show confidence score

Treatment Suggestions

- Recommended fertilizers
- Natural remedies
- Prevention tips

Disease Library

- Search diseases by crop
- View symptoms and causes
- Read treatment guides

Reports

- Save scan history
- View previous disease reports
