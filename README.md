

🌱 AgroV - Smart Agriculture Assistant

AgroV is an AI-powered agriculture assistant designed to help farmers detect plant diseases, improve crop health, increase productivity, and make informed farming decisions.

The application combines Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, and Agricultural Data Analytics to provide practical solutions for modern farming while remaining accessible to farmers in rural areas.

Natural Alternatives

📷 Application Workflow

1. Farmer captures a plant image.
2. AI detects disease from the image.
3. Disease information is displayed.
4. Treatment recommendations are provided.
5. Suitable fertilizers are suggested.
6. Organic alternatives are recommended.
7. Nearby agricultural stores are displayed.
8. Reports are saved for future reference.
